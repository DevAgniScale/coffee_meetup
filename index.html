07 · How Our Agent Works
💬 Built for Discord

From question to answer, step by step

STEP 1

Student asks a question on Discord.

STEP 2

Discord Bot receives the message.

STEP 3

AI reads course_info.txt for real course data.

STEP 4

Question + info are sent to Gemini.

STEP 5

If found, Gemini answers from the course file.

STEP 6

If not found, it uses general knowledge instead.

STEP 7

Asked for API keys or secrets? AI politely refuses.

STEP 8

Answer is sent back to the student on Discord.

09 · Before We Write Code

Plan first. Prompt second.

✕

Most people just say "build me a bot." But AI doesn't know the purpose, the workflow, or the rules unless you tell it. Good developers define the problem, design the workflow, pick the tools — then write the prompt. Clear planning = better AI output. That's Prompt Engineering.
